Boris Cherny — the guy who created Claude Code — described his actual workflow: "As I'm coding, as my Claudes are coding in a loop, they will communicate over Slack to talk to other people's Claudes that are also running in a loop."

Not one agent doing everything. Multiple specialized agents, each in their own loop, coordinating through a channel that already has permissions and an audit log: Slack.

That's the Shadow Org pattern, in production, from the person who built the tool. The interesting part isn't the multi-agent bit — it's that he picked a channel humans already govern instead of inventing a new one.
