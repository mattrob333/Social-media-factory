Google shipped environment hooks for Gemini Managed Agents today.

Block, lint, enforce rules on agent behavior before it reaches your systems.

This is the quietest important launch of the week. Everyone will be talking about Crew Studio (and they should). But Google just gave every enterprise deploying agents a way to say "no" before the action happens.

An environment hook is a guardrail that fires before the agent acts. It can block, lint, or transform the behavior before it reaches production.

Most agent platforms are missing this layer entirely. Agents act, you find out later. Google just made "find out later" optional.

If you're deploying managed agents anywhere, this is the feature that separates a demo from a deployment.
