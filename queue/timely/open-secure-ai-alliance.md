40 companies — NVIDIA, Microsoft, SpaceX, HPE, Palantir, HPE, Nous Research — just launched the Open Secure AI Alliance for open agent security tooling.

OpenAI and Google are notably absent. That's a signal.

The alliance is building real infrastructure: NOOA for agent harnesses, SPIFFE/SPIRE for zero-trust identity, MDASH for scanning. All necessary.

But there's a governance gap nobody's addressing: who approved the agent's deployment in the first place?

Security tools catch the breach. Governance prevents the conditions for one.

Your agent has credentials, permissions, and system access. Where's its manager? Where's the audit trail? Segregation of duties doesn't stop mattering because the employee is a model.

Glad @NousResearch is at this table. Next step: an NHI governance working group.
