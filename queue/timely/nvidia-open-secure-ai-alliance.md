NVIDIA just launched the Open Secure AI Alliance — 40+ firms including CrowdStrike, Microsoft, Palantir, and notably OpenClaw and Nous Research.

The post explicitly says: "An AI agent isn't just a language model. It's a complex system built from models, harnesses, and guardrails."

That's the thesis I've been beating the drum on for a year. Agent security lives in the harness and the identity layer, not in the model weights. NVIDIA, the world's most important AI infrastructure company, just formalized this as a coalition.

NOOA (their new open-source agent framework) makes agent behavior testable, traceable, and auditable — exactly the governance primitives every forward-deployed team needs before scaling agents past a handful.

Meta is conspicuously absent. The alliance exists because of the Hugging Face breach two weeks ago, where closed tools blocked forensic work and only open weights saved the analysis.

The model race is still running. The security race just got organized.
